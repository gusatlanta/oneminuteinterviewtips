---
title: How to answer "Why are you leaving?"
slug: why-are-you-leaving
date: 2026-11-20
summary: Keep the answer short and pointed forward. One honest reason, then what you want next, then why this role fits.
---

Interviewers ask this to see how you talk about a change, not to catch you out.

Keep it short and point forward. Give one honest reason, then what you want next, then why this role fits that.

Good reasons are simple: you want a larger scope, a different kind of problem, a team or setting that matches how you work, or a chapter that ended, such as a contract, a restructure, or a move. You do not need to share every detail.

Do not badmouth a manager, a team, or the last job. Even if the exit was messy, stay factual and brief. If you were let go, say so in one plain sentence, then move to what you learned and what you are looking for now.

Avoid making "I was bored" or "for more money" the whole answer. You can want growth and fair pay. Tie it to the work you want to do.

Close by connecting your reason to this job, so the answer does not trail off.
