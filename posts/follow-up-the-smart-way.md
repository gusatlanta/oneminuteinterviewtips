Most candidates apply and wait. Don't.

Three to five business days after you apply, find the hiring manager on LinkedIn and send two lines: the job you applied for, and one result you've delivered that fits it.

Keep it short. Name the role in the first line so they know why you're writing. In the second line, pick one result that matches what the job is asking for. A number helps when you have one: time you cut, a target you hit, or a problem you closed.

Skip the long recap of your background. They can open your application for that. And skip "just checking in." It asks them to do the work. A specific note gives them something to reply to.

If you cannot find the hiring manager, a recruiter or the person who posted the role is the next best contact. Same two lines.

One message is enough. If they do not reply, leave it there and keep your search moving.
