---
title: Tell me about yourself in 60 seconds
slug: tell-me-about-yourself-in-60-seconds
date: 2026-11-21
summary: Use three beats, present, past, and future, and keep the answer to one calm minute.
---

"Tell me about yourself" is not a life story. It is a 60-second path from who you are at work to why you are sitting in this interview.

Use three beats.

Present: one sentence on what you do now and who you help.

Past: one or two results that prove you can do this job. Pick proof that matches the posting, not your full history.

Future: one sentence on why this role is the logical next step.

Write it down and say it out loud with a timer. Most first drafts run long. Cut until it fits in a minute at a calm pace. Leave a beat at the end so the interviewer can jump in.

Skip personal history unless they ask. Skip a tour of every job on your resume. The goal is a clear through-line, not a complete record.

Practice until you can say it without reading. Then stop memorizing the exact words, so it still sounds like you.
