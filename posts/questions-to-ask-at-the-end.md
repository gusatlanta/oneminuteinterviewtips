---
title: Questions to ask at the end
slug: questions-to-ask-at-the-end
date: 2026-11-19
summary: Ask two or three questions that show you want to understand the work, then listen and follow one of them up.
---

When they ask if you have questions, they are still interviewing you. Use the time to learn how the work actually gets done.

Ask two or three. Listen to the answer, then follow one of them up. A real exchange beats a long list you read off a page.

Three solid questions for almost any interview:

- What would success look like in the first 90 days?
- What does this team rely on each other for in a normal week?
- What is the hardest part of this role that is not in the job post?

Skip questions whose answers are already on the posting or the career page. Save pay, time off, and benefits for later, once there is mutual interest, unless they open that door first.

Write your questions down before you go in. At the end of an interview it is easy to go blank. A short list keeps you ready without making the conversation feel scripted.
